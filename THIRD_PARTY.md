## Pattern sources

The catalogues also draw on [Wikipedia: Signs of AI writing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing), the Russian Wikipedia essay [Признаки сгенерированности текста](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Википедия:Признаки_сгенерированности_текста) and the book «Пиши, сокращай» by Maxim Ilyakhov. Several before and after examples in `references/patterns-en.md` come from the English Wikipedia page by way of blader/humanizer.
